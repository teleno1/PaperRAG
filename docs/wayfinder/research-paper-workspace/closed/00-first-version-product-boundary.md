# First-Version Product Boundary

## Question

What product, user, evidence boundary, report experience, and non-goals define
the first version of PaperRAG?

## Resolution

PaperRAG will become a trustworthy research-paper reading and reporting
workspace for individual researchers and graduate students. A user creates a
workspace with a topic, then uploads papers or searches for Candidate Papers.
Only user-selected papers can be parsed, indexed, retrieved, or cited.

Online discovery uses open academic indexes and automatically imports only
publicly downloadable PDFs. Restricted papers remain metadata plus an external
link unless the user uploads an authorised file.

The user chooses Chinese or English, receives an editable outline before body
generation, then edits the generated Literature Report in the browser. A
supported sentence or bullet can cite one or more source chunks; selecting its
citation opens paper title, page or section, and source excerpt. A substantive
edit to a cited claim puts its citation into pending review until it is kept,
removed, or refreshed.

The first version is single-user and exports Markdown. Multi-user
collaboration, PDF/Word export, and automatic restricted-content ingestion are
out of scope.
