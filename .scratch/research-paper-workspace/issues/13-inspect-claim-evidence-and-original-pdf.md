# 13 - Inspect Claim Evidence and Open the Original PDF

**What to build:** A researcher can focus a Claim in the writing stage, inspect
every cited source in the Task Detail Pane, and open a selected source at its
page in the authorised original PDF.

**Blocked by:** 12 - Publish an Editable Evidence-Driven Literature Report.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A cited Claim has the accepted hover affordance and persistent selected
  highlight; the Task Detail Pane shows all cited papers, versions, excerpts,
  page/section locations, and Citation Review States for one or multiple
  sources.
- [ ] Selecting a source opens the exact authorised original PDF at its anchor;
  unavailable historical originals show their true unavailable state, retained
  citation history, and recovery/refresh action without chunk-assembled paper
  content.
- [ ] The interface distinguishes verified, pending-review, removed, and
  evidence-unavailable citations without presenting historical evidence as
  current support.
- [ ] Persistence, API, browser interaction, and a multi-source acceptance test
  cover focus, source inspection, PDF page navigation, and unavailable-source
  recovery.
