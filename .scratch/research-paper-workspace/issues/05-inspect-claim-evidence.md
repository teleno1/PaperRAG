# 05 — Inspect Claim Evidence and Multiple Sources

**What to build:** A researcher can select a Claim Citation in the Literature
Report and inspect its evidence in a side panel. The panel shows every cited
Research Paper, available page or section location, source excerpt, and the
claim's current citation state.

**Blocked by:** Wayfinder decisions: Audit stable PDF source-location anchors;
Prototype the report editor and evidence-trace interaction; 04 — Generate an
Editable Cited Literature Report.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A report claim can visibly reference one or multiple Source Chunks.
- [ ] Selecting its Claim Citation shows paper identity, source provenance,
  excerpt, and page and/or section information when available.
- [ ] The side panel clearly distinguishes verified, pending-review, removed,
  or unavailable evidence states.
- [ ] The interaction is covered by an acceptance test using a claim supported
  by more than one source.
