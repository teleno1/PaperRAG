# 09 — Prototype the Final Research Workspace Experience

**What to build:** A researcher can exercise a disposable, interactive
prototype of the complete Research Workspace experience: preparing Research
Papers, approving a Report Outline, editing a Literature Report, inspecting
Claim Evidence, reviewing citations, and exporting Markdown. The prototype
gives the Workspace Owner a concrete basis to accept the final information
architecture and interaction model.

**Blocked by:** 08 — Export Markdown and Demonstrate the Product Workflow.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The prototype represents all user-visible states and recovery paths from
  delivery tickets 01 through 08, including Evidence Readiness, Evidence
  Coverage, Citation Review State, and evidence gaps.
- [ ] The Workspace Owner can inspect the principal desktop and responsive
  flows, record decisions, and explicitly accept the information architecture,
  panel behaviour, and interaction model.
- [ ] The prototype is disposable and uses controlled local fixture state; it
  neither changes production APIs or persistence nor substitutes mocked output
  for product acceptance.
- [ ] Its accepted decisions are recorded as the implementation input for 10.
